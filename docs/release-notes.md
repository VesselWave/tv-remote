Wave Remote now remembers more than one TV.

- Switch TVs or add another with the button next to the remote's settings; each TV keeps its own pairing and wake-up details
- Give a TV its own name on this phone: tap the pencil next to it in the TV list
- The input list says whether something is connected to each port, and whether it's sending a signal

Installs over 0.3 and keeps your pairing. To check the download: `sha256sum --check wave-remote-v0.4.apk.sha256`.

Tested with an LG 65UT7570PUB (webOS 24) and a Pixel 7. Setup and troubleshooting are in the [README](https://github.com/VesselWave/wave-remote#readme). Not affiliated with LG.
