Rebuilt with Expo and React Native. The layout and the TV protocol engine are the same as in 0.1.

- Icons on the remote's buttons
- Material menus for setup, settings, apps, inputs and the keyboard; apps are searchable
- Wider volume and channel rockers and a bigger touchpad
- Runs on Android 7+ (was 9+); arm64 only

Installs over 0.1 and keeps your pairing. To check the download: `sha256sum --check tv-remote-v0.2.apk.sha256`.

Tested with an LG 65UT7570PUB (webOS 24) and a Pixel 7. Setup and troubleshooting are in the [README](https://github.com/VesselWave/wave-remote#readme). Not affiliated with LG.
