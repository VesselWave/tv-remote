Wave Remote 0.5 is smaller and asks for less.

- The APK is about 13 MB, down from 31 MB
- No more "display over other apps" or storage permissions; the app never used them
- A TV paired over an encrypted connection is never contacted unencrypted again, so nobody on your network can force a downgrade. Older TVs that only support unencrypted connections still work
- Looking for TVs only reads device details from the device that answered

Installs over 0.4 and keeps your pairing. To check the download: `sha256sum --check wave-remote-v0.5.apk.sha256`.

Tested with an LG 65UT7570PUB (webOS 24) and a Pixel 7. Setup and troubleshooting are in the [README](https://github.com/VesselWave/wave-remote#readme). Not affiliated with LG.
