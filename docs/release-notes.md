Wave Remote 0.6 reconnects when your TV comes back.

- Reconnects automatically to a paired TV while the app is open, including when you turn the TV on with its own remote
- Disconnected controls dim consistently, with power still lit
- Fixes overlapping touchpad hint text while disconnected
- Prepares release builds for F-Droid rebuild verification using Node.js and Gradle

Installs over 0.5 and keeps your pairing. To check the download: `sha256sum --check wave-remote-v0.6.apk.sha256`.

Protocol and app tests cover the changes. Physical-device testing of 0.6 has not yet been performed. Setup and troubleshooting are in the [README](https://github.com/VesselWave/wave-remote#readme). Not affiliated with LG.
