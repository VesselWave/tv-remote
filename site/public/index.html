Everything the remote in the couch cushions does.

  • Buttons that feel right

    A big D-pad that speeds up while you hold it. Volume and channel repeat smoothly until you let go.

  • Touchpad with scrolling

    Glide the TV pointer, tap to click, and scroll with two fingers or the strip on the right edge.

  • Your volume keys work

    Press your phone’s own volume buttons to turn the TV up or down, even while you browse.

  • Apps and inputs

    Search your TV’s apps and open one with a tap. Switch to a console or set-top box just as fast.

  • Type with your phone

    No more hunting letters with arrows. Send search text straight into the field on your TV.

  • Turns the TV on, too

    After you connect once, wake the TV over Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Power off is one tap away.

Stays on your network.

No sign-in, no cloud service, no analytics and no ads. Your phone pairs with your TV once, keeps that pairing to itself, and checks it’s the same TV every time it connects.

Set up in a minute.

  1. Install

    Download the APK on your phone and open it. Android will ask you to allow installs from your browser the first time.

  2. Pick your TV

    Open TV Remote while your phone and TV share Wi-Fi. It finds the TV for you, or you can enter its IP address.

  3. Approve once

    Accept the request that appears on the TV, using its own remote. From then on it reconnects by itself.

Want to switch the TV on from your phone? Turn on Turn on via Wi-Fi (or Mobile TV On) in your TV’s settings.
